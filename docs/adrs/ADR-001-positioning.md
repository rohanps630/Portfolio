# ADR-001 — Positioning: full-stack engineer specializing in production AI systems

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-06-11

## Context
Resume evidence spans full-stack/mobile platform work (POS at scale, team lead) and production AI work (multi-agent platform, AI code reviewer with evals/guardrails). Current site tagline ("Web, mobile, and AI — built to ship") positions as a generalist; the AI market is split between unproven "AI engineers" and full-stack engineers with no AI depth.

## Decision
Position at the intersection: **"Full-stack product engineer who ships production AI systems"**, hero line "I build AI systems that survive production." Three proof pillars (agentic systems in production, self-built AI depth, scale-grade product engineering) govern all content prioritization.

## Alternatives
1. *Stay generalist* — safe, but commoditized; wastes the rare AI evidence.
2. *Pure "AI Engineer"* — abandons 4 years of differentiating platform evidence; invites hype skepticism.
3. *"Senior Full Stack · AI/LLM Integration" (resume header verbatim)* — accurate but additive; weaker narrative pull.

## Tradeoffs
Sharper positioning narrows appeal to non-AI full-stack roles and some freelance clients (mitigated: Tier-2 client work and typed contact path remain). "Production" claim raises the proof bar — the site must actually be disciplined (budgets, a11y, CI), which costs effort.

## Consequences
All copy, IA ordering, and project tiering derive from the pillars; contradictory signals (LangChain/ChatGPT skill chips, 4.75-years stat) must be removed; the two missing enterprise case studies become mandatory content work.
